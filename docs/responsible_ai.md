# MATS — Responsible AI, Fairness & Bias Audit Governance

## 1. Principles of Responsible Financial AI
MATS adheres to rigorous Responsible AI governance tailored to retail investor protection:
1. **Factual Grounding & Zero Fabrication**:
   - Agent syntheses are strictly bound to audited SEC Form 10-K filings, continuous OHLCV market feeds, and deterministic mathematical risk factors.
   - When data is missing, the system outputs `"Unavailable"` rather than generating plausible hallucinations.

2. **Confirmation Bias Defense (Devil's Advocate Engine)**:
   - Retail investors frequently fall prey to echo-chamber confirmation bias.
   - For every bullish thesis generated, the `CounterargumentAgent` automatically synthesizes structural bear-case challenges, accounting vulnerabilities, and valuation headwinds.

3. **Multilingual Fairness & Non-English Parity**:
   - Financial intelligence is traditionally accessible only to fluent English speakers.
   - MATS provides first-class support for Tamil (`தமிழ்`) and Hindi (`हिन्दी`) without degrading analytical depth.
   - Protected financial glossaries ensure concepts like *drawdown* or *operating margin* are paired bilingually.

4. **Senior-Friendly Accessibility & Inclusion**:
   - Dynamic scaling from Normal to Extra Large (+4px) text.
   - High-contrast WCAG AAA borders and text contrast.
   - Reduced motion modes for vestibular safety.
   - Screen-reader accessible alternatives for every visual chart.

5. **Human-in-the-Loop Sovereignty**:
   - The user retains total decision-making autonomy.
   - Voice commands cannot execute destructive or sensitive actions (e.g. portfolio purging requires explicit UI modal confirmation).
